副教授｜臺北醫學大學 護理學院 學士後護理學系

Associate Professor, Post-Baccalaureate Program in Nursing, College of Nursing, Taipei Medical University

致力於慢性阻塞性肺病（COPD）病人照護、肺復健與慢性病護理，並結合大型健康資料庫分析、系統性回顧與質性研究，以及以實證為基礎的護理教育創新。

My work focuses on the care of people living with COPD, pulmonary rehabilitation and chronic illness nursing — drawing on large health-database analysis, systematic reviews, qualitative inquiry, and evidence-based innovation in nursing education.
